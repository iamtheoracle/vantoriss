# Vantoris Group Identity Assets

The public identity system uses the approved visual direction: midnight navy, warm ivory, antique brass and Instrument Serif display typography, paired with Inter for readable interface text.

## Included artwork

- vantoris-emblem.svg — standalone geometric V emblem.
- vantoris-wordmark-horizontal.svg — primary light-background wordmark.
- vantoris-wordmark-inverse.svg — dark-background wordmark.
- vantoris-wordmark-vertical.svg — vertical lockup.
- vantoris-seal.svg — group seal without an invented establishment date.
- vantoris-app-icon.svg — browser and application icon.
- vantoris-letterhead.svg — official correspondence layout artwork.
- vantoris-presentation-cover.svg — presentation cover artwork.
- vantoris-social-card.svg — social and publication card artwork.
- vantoris-wordmark-monochrome.svg — single-colour lockup.
- vantoris-staff-portal.svg — staff portal identity lockup.
- vantoris-client-portal.svg — client portal identity lockup.

## Usage boundaries

These are original Vantoris SVG assets. The group wordmark is used by the new public company website. Existing member and operations portal branding is intentionally not replaced until the exact relationship between that application and the Vantoris Private Institution organisation is confirmed.

The Hambros Bank historical tribute is a separate historical collection and must never be presented as Vantoris branding or as evidence of ownership, succession or affiliation.

The letterhead and presentation cover are visual templates, not proof of legal registration, authority, address, regulatory status or approval to issue a document.
